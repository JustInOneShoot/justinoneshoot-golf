How it works

You already have the photos. We do the rest.

  1. Choose a package

    Pay with your package's "Order now" button below, then email us your photos.

  2. Send your photos

    Email a Google Drive or Dropbox link to abhiram@justinoneshoot.com with your course name and address: 3-5 photos per hole, aerials if you have them, and your scorecard. Add your logo and any notes, like the music style or what to feature. Please only send photos you own or have permission to use, for example from your photographer.

  3. Get your video

    Horizontal for your website and YouTube, vertical for Instagram and TikTok, within 3 business days of receiving usable photos.

Honest by design

Our flyovers are AI visualisations animated from your course's own photos, not surveyed drone footage.

We add camera motion, music and text. We never add, remove or change bunkers, water, trees or greens, and yardages come only from your scorecard.

Questions

What photos do you need?

At least 3-5 photos per hole (tee, fairway, green), aerial or drone photos if you have them, and a course map or scorecard with layouts and yardages. A shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder is easiest.

How long does it take?

Within 3 business days of receiving usable photos.

Is it real drone footage?

No. It's an AI visualisation made from your photos, so there's no crew on site and no closed holes. If you have your own drone photos, send them and we'll use them.

Can I get changes?

Yes, one round of revisions is included. Reply to the delivery email with what you'd like changed.

What do I receive?

MP4 files: horizontal (16:9) for your website and YouTube, and vertical (9:16) for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

How do I pay?

Through the payment link for your package. Payments are handled securely by Stripe.
