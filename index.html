Honest by design

Our flyovers are AI visualisations animated from your course's own photos, not surveyed drone footage. We add camera motion, music and text; we never add, remove or change bunkers, water, trees or greens. Yardages come only from your scorecard.

Questions

What photos do you need?

At least 3-5 photos per hole (tee, fairway, green), aerial or drone photos if you have them, and a course map or scorecard with layouts and yardages. A shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder is easiest.

How long does it take?

Within 3 business days of receiving usable photos.

Is it real drone footage?

No. It's an AI visualisation made from your photos, so there's no crew on site and no closed holes. If you have your own drone photos, send them and we'll use them.

Can I get changes?

Yes, one round of revisions is included. Reply to the delivery email with what you'd like changed.

What do I receive?

MP4 files: horizontal (16:9) for your website and YouTube, and vertical (9:16) for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

How do I pay?

Through the payment link for your package (the "Order now" button above). Payments are handled securely by Stripe.
