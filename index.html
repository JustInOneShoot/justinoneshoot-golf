How it works

You already have the photos. We do the rest, with no crew on your course and no holes closed.

  1. Choose a package

    Pay with your package's "Order now" button below, then email us your photos.

  2. Send your photos

    Email a Google Drive or Dropbox link to abhiram@justinoneshoot.com with your course name and address: 3-5 photos per hole, any aerials and your scorecard. Add your logo and any notes. Please only send photos you own or have permission to use, for example from your photographer.

  3. Get your video

    Horizontal and vertical versions within 24 hours of receiving usable photos.

Honest by design

AI visualisations animated from your own photos, not drone footage.

We add motion, music and text. We never alter bunkers, water, trees or greens. Yardages come only from your scorecard.

Questions

What photos do you need?

3-5 per hole (tee, fairway, green), any aerials, and your scorecard or course map. A shared Google Drive or Dropbox folder works best.

How long does it take?

24 hours from usable photos, any day of the week.

Is it real drone footage?

No. It's an AI visualisation of your photos: no crew, no closed holes. Send drone photos if you have them.

Can I get changes?

Yes. One round of revisions is included.

What do I receive?

MP4s: 16:9 for your website and YouTube, 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

How do I pay?

Through the payment link for your package. Payments are handled securely by Stripe.
